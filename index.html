03 / Research in progress

A better memory starts
with better questions.

How should a personal AI retrieve and inspect long-term multimodal history to make better predictions about what someone will do next?

I’m building reproducible evaluation infrastructure to investigate that question. The engineering is implemented; long-term comparative evaluation is ongoing.

Read the research direction

01 / RETRIEVAL

Find the relevant episode.

Which moment in a large personal history actually matters to this prediction?
