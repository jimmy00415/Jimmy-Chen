Biography

I’m Jimmy Chen, an AI engineer, startup founder, and independent agent-systems researcher. I’m interested in how AI systems can become more reliable, more grounded in evidence, and more useful in everyday life.

I lead Simplify as Founder & CEO, working with our team on product direction, technology, and execution. We build MatchaCode for AI-assisted coding and Flash Launch for publishing software.

Alongside that, I contribute to open source—including a merged OpenClaw compatibility repair—and investigate episode retrieval and multimodal evidence for personal AI memory.

My engineering experience spans grounded retrieval at Motion Expert, ESG data systems at Climind, and research at Hong Kong Baptist University.

Research interests

I’m investigating how a personal AI should retrieve and inspect long-term multimodal history to make better predictions about what someone will do next.

Episode retrieval

How do we find the relevant moment in a large personal history, rather than simply give a model more context?

Evidence inspection

What changes when the model can inspect original text and visual evidence instead of relying only on a compressed account?

I built the data and evaluation foundation; comparative evaluation is ongoing. The recorded data layer covers 1.4M events, with NextAct prediction points using the external NextMe-800 and EgoLife resources.
